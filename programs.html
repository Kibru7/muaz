Primary Education

Our primary curriculum focuses on building strong foundational skills in literacy, numeracy, and critical thinking. We emphasize interactive learning with hands-on activities that engage students in the learning process.

Library

Our library provides students with a calm space for reading, research, and independent learning. It supports academic growth with a wide range of books and study materials.

Science & Technology

Our science program includes modern laboratory facilities where students conduct experiments and develop scientific inquiry skills. Technology integration enhances learning across all subjects.

ICT Center

Our ICT Center equips students with digital literacy, computer skills, and practical technology experience that support learning across all subjects.
