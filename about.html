About Our School

Nurturing Minds in Nefas Selak Lafto

Located in Nefas Selak Lafto Sub-city, Woreda 6, Addis Ababa, netsanet chora primary and middle school is a government institution recognized for its high performance since 2017.

We provide complete learning resources, extensive library services, and a comfortable learning environment designed to foster academic excellence and personal growth.
